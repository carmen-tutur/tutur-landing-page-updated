These offer terms apply only to Founding Family members, in addition to the Tutur Terms & Conditions (tutur.my/terms). If they differ, these offer terms apply to Founding Families.

F1. **Who.** Registration opened on 16 October 2026 and the app launches on 26 October 2026. The offer is for the first 20 families who register by 30 November 2026, or until all 20 places are taken. Some places are reserved for families referred by our partner centres with a valid code.

F2. **Free until 31 December 2026.** Founding Families use Tutur free from the day their account is set up until 31 December 2026. No payment details are needed during this time.

F3. **Founding price from 1 January 2027.** You can then continue for RM19 a month. This price is locked for as long as your subscription stays active. It includes new modules (such as Module 2) and features added to the standard Tutur app, even if the standard price goes up.

F4. **Nothing is charged automatically.** We will remind you before your free period ends. You are charged only if you choose to continue. If you don't, your access pauses, and you can rejoin later at the standard price.

F5. **Your part.** In return for free access, you agree to complete three short feedback check-ins (around week 2, around month 1, and before the end of December) and one call of about 15 minutes with our team. If a family does not take part, we may end its free period early with 7 days' notice.

F6. **Not included.** Add-on services, such as sessions with a speech therapist, Tutur Coach workshops or any future premium plan, are charged separately.

F7. **Early access.** You can try Module 2 before it is released to everyone.

F8. **Keeping your price.** You lose the founding price if you cancel, or if your subscription lapses for more than 14 days after 1 January 2027. If you rejoin later, the standard price applies.

F9. **One place per family.** Places and rewards are non-transferable and have no cash value.

F10. **Closing the offer.** We will close the offer once 20 places are taken or on 30 November 2026, whichever comes first. Closing it does not affect families who have already joined.
