Tutur Technologies PLT (Co. Reg. No. 202604000984 / LLP0046213-LGN) ("Tutur", "we", "us") runs Tutur, a parent education app that helps parents and caregivers support their child's speech and language development at home. Tutur does not screen, diagnose, assess or treat any condition, and is not a speech-language therapy service. This notice explains how we handle your personal data and your child's under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA). It is available in English and Bahasa Melayu.

## 1. Who this notice covers

It covers parents and legal guardians who create a Tutur account, and the children they register. Tutur is used by a parent or guardian on the child's behalf. We do not knowingly allow children to open their own accounts.

## 2. What data we collect

### a) About you (parent or guardian)

- Your name, email address, phone number and relationship to your child, which you give us when you sign up.
- If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile photo, shared by Google with your permission.
- Payment records from our payment provider: the amount, date, payment status and last 4 digits of your card. We never see your full card number.

### b) About your child

- Your child's name or nickname, date of birth or age, and the language(s) spoken at home.
- **Sensitive information:** details about your child's speech, language or development, any diagnosis or therapy history you choose to share, and their progress in the app.

### c) Collected automatically

- Device type, app version, activity logs and error reports, to keep the app working.

### d) New features

We are developing new features, such as speech activities that may use your child's voice. If a new feature needs a new type of data, we will tell you first and ask for your consent before collecting it. You can say no and still use Tutur.

## 3. Sensitive personal data

Information about a child's speech, language, development or health is sensitive personal data under the PDPA. We process it only with your explicit consent, which we ask for separately at sign-up. You can withdraw it at any time (see section 8).

## 4. Why we use it

- To create and manage your account, including Google sign-in.
- To personalise activities to your child's level and show you their progress.
- To process subscription fees and keep payment records.
- To provide support and send you service messages about your account and subscription.
- To improve Tutur, using data that is de-identified wherever possible.
- To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your child's data for advertising. If you sign in with Google, we use your Google data only to sign you in and set up your account.

## 5. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers who help us run Tutur, under contracts that require them to protect it:

- **Data storage:** secure cloud database and storage providers.
- **Sign-in:** Google, if you choose to sign in with your Google account.
- **Payments:** licensed payment processors, who handle your card details for us.
- **Hosting:** providers that host our website and app.
- **App features:** technology providers that help us run and improve the app, including speech or AI tools where we use them.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it. We do not share your child's data with schools, therapists or other third parties unless you ask us to.

## 6. Data processed outside Malaysia

Some providers above store or process data outside Malaysia. We transfer data only to providers that protect it to a standard at least equivalent to the PDPA, as the law allows.

## 7. How long we keep it

We keep your and your child's data while your account is active and for 6 months after you close it, then delete it or remove anything that identifies you. If an account is not used for 12 months, we will email you before deleting it. Payment records are kept for 7 years, as Malaysian tax law requires.

## 8. Your rights

You can, for yourself and your child:

- ask for a copy of the data we hold;
- ask us to correct it;
- withdraw consent, or ask us to stop or limit processing;
- ask us to transfer your data to another service, where technically feasible;
- ask us to delete your account and your child's data.

Email hello@tutur.my. We will reply within 21 days. A fee may apply to access requests where the PDPA allows it.

## 9. Is giving data required?

Parent name, email and your child's name and age are required to provide Tutur. Developmental information is optional, but without it Tutur cannot personalise activities or track progress as well. If you refuse or withdraw consent for required data, we may not be able to provide Tutur.

## 10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to team members who need it. If a data breach is likely to harm you, we will notify you and the Personal Data Protection Commissioner as the law requires.

## 11. Changes to this notice

We will post any update on this page with a new version number and date. If a change affects how we use your child's data, we will tell you by email before it takes effect.
